## Full Description

**SoCreate** — Animate Your Imagination

Professional-grade drawing and animation studio, built from the ground up for the **Samsung Galaxy Tab S10+** and optimized for S Pen.

### ✨ Key Features

**Drawing & Painting**
- 13 premium brush presets with pressure curves
- Velocity-based brush settings & stabilizer
- 28+ blend modes
- Smart shape detection (8 types)
- Symmetry drawing (7 modes)
- Reference images & numeric keypad input

**Advanced Animation**
- Multi-track timeline with variable frame durations
- Enhanced onion skin (8 modes)
- Motion trails, ghost frames & multi-frame selection
- Batch editing, frame tags & cel linking
- Audio tracks + voice recording
- Rotoscoping support

**Puppet Mesh & Deformation**
- Full puppet warp with triangulated mesh
- Bone hierarchy with IK
- 4 rig presets (Humanoid, Quadruped, Face, Hand)
- Vertex weight painting & pose interpolation
- Liquify tools

**Layer System**
- Unlimited layers with 16+ blend modes
- Clipping masks, layer effects & outlining
- Adjustment layers & groups

**Samsung Tab S10+ Optimizations**
- 120Hz rendering with Choreographer
- Full S Pen support (4096 pressure levels, tilt, hover, button)
- Display P3 wide gamut
- Samsung DeX support
- Edge-to-edge immersive mode

**Connectivity & Sharing**
- Google Sign-In (YouTube upload up to 4K)
- artistso.com integration
- User-owned crash reports

### Privacy
All artwork stays on your device. No data collection. Optional opt-in crash reporting only.
